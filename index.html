ABOUT US

A trading partner for the materials that build Telangana.

Sri Manikanta Traders is a Hyderabad-based partnership firm in the business of coal and minerals trading — the sale and purchase of coal, fly ash and pond ash. We connect the sources of these materials, from mines and Telangana's power plants, with the cement factories and industries that depend on them.

The firm is registered under GST as a regular taxpayer, with its principal place of business in the TSIIC industrial colony at IDA Jeedimetla. Both partners are actively involved in day-to-day operations, so customers deal directly with the people running the business.

Fly ash and pond ash are by-products of coal-fired power generation. Putting them to work in cement, concrete and bricks reduces the need for fresh raw materials and keeps ash out of storage ponds — good for our customers' costs and for the environment.

WHAT WE SUPPLY

Three materials, one dependable supplier.

01

Coal

Sourced from mines and supplied as fuel for cement kilns, boilers and other industrial heating needs.

  • Cement plant kilns
  • Industrial boilers
  • Process heating
02

Fly Ash

The fine powder collected from flue gases at thermal power plants. A key ingredient in Portland Pozzolana Cement and a partial cement replacement in concrete.

  • PPC cement manufacturing
  • Ready-mix concrete
  • Fly ash bricks and blocks
03

Pond Ash

The coarser ash mix stored in ash ponds at power stations. An economical fill and construction material.

  • Road embankments and fill
  • Land development and backfilling
  • Bricks and blocks
